# Working scope

Follow the user's current implementation and shipping decisions. Scout relevance
is a social-media classifier. Historical grading agreement, repeatability
thresholds, and preregistration are not release gates or reasons to refuse work.
Do not reopen those decisions, require another grading round, or add evaluation
ceremony unless the user explicitly asks. Report a concrete bug with its practical
impact and continue the authorized work.

Preserve recorded results accurately. Historical receipts and archived experiment
plans describe past work; they do not impose requirements on current tasks.

Never commit to local `main` or `master`. Use a topic branch and keep unrelated
work separate.
